# Enterprise Vendor Procurement, SLA & Risk Review (FY26)

## 1. Executive Summary & Vendor Tiering
The Global Procurement Committee oversees 14 strategic cloud, hardware, and service vendors. Vendors are classified into three risk tiers based on business criticality, data exposure risk, and annual contractual commitment.

## 2. Risk Classification Framework
- Tier 1 Strategic (Annual Spend > $10,000,000 or Direct Production Access): Requires quarterly executive business reviews (QBRs), SOC 2 Type II attestation annually, third-party penetration testing results, and dedicated primary/secondary escalation engineers.
- Tier 2 Operational (Annual Spend between $1,000,000 and $10,000,000): Semi-annual performance audit, ISO 27001 certification, standard 4-hour critical issue SLA.
- Tier 3 Commodity (Annual Spend < $1,000,000): Annual contract renewal evaluation, standard commercial terms.

## 3. Mandatory Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
3.1 Uptime Commitments:
- Core cloud infrastructure providers must guarantee 99.99% monthly availability (maximum permissible unplanned downtime: 4.38 minutes per calendar month).
- Failure to meet availability commitments incurs financial penalty credits: 10% invoice discount for 99.90%–99.98%, and 25% discount for < 99.90% uptime.

3.2 Payment Terms:
- Standard enterprise payment schedule is Net 60 days from validated invoice receipt.
- Strategic suppliers with early discount clauses (2% 10 Net 30) are prioritized by the treasury team for automated clearing house (ACH) settlement.

## 4. Contract Termination & Exit Protocols
- Termination for Convenience: The enterprise reserves unilateral authority to terminate any vendor agreement with 60 days written notice without early termination penalties.
- Data Return & Deletion: Upon contract termination, the vendor must provide verified cryptographically erased confirmation of all proprietary datasets within 30 calendar days.
