# Global Enterprise Information Security & Compliance Policy (Standard 402.B)

## 1. Scope & Governance
This policy establishes mandatory data protection and access control standards across all enterprise business units, regional subsidiaries, and cloud infrastructure environments. Compliance is audited semi-annually by the Internal Audit and Risk Management committee.

## 2. Authentication & Identity Management
2.1 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):
- Hardware-token or FIDO2-compliant phishing-resistant MFA is strictly required for all administrative access to production systems, code repositories, and financial ledgers.
- Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) is permitted as a secondary method for standard employee workstation access. SMS-based verification is deprecated and strictly forbidden.
- Session timeout for administrative consoles is enforced at 15 minutes of inactivity. Standard corporate portal sessions expire after 8 hours.

## 3. Data Classification & Retention Protocols
3.1 Classification Levels:
- Tier 1 (Confidential / Restricted): Personally Identifiable Information (PII), customer financial records, cryptographic keys, and executive strategic plans.
- Tier 2 (Internal Business Use): Internal project documentation, operational telemetry, non-public roadmaps, and employee rosters.
- Tier 3 (Public): Published marketing collateral, press releases, and approved open-source repositories.

3.2 Mandatory Retention Periods:
- Financial and accounting transaction logs must be retained in immutable, write-once-read-many (WORM) storage for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with SOX guidelines.
- Access audit logs and security telemetry must be preserved for 365 days in active searchable storage and archived for an additional 2 years.
- Customer support call transcripts and voice recordings must be permanently purged or anonymized within 90 days of issue closure unless subject to active legal hold.

## 4. Encryption Standards
4.1 Data in Transit:
- All external and inter-service HTTP communications must enforce TLS 1.3 encryption with strict cipher suites (AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305). Deprecated TLS 1.0 and 1.1 protocols are permanently blocked at the ingress gateway.

4.2 Data at Rest:
- All database volumes, block stores, object stores, and local developer laptops must enforce AES-256 encryption. Master encryption keys are rotated automatically every 90 days through an isolated Hardware Security Module (HSM).

## 5. Incident Response & Escalation Cadence
- Severity 1 (Critical Security Breach / Active Data Exfiltration): Security Operations Center (SOC) must initiate triage within 15 minutes of detection. Executive breach notification must be submitted to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) within 60 minutes.
- Severity 2 (Major Service Degradation / Suspected Compromise): Triage initiated within 1 hour; resolution plan finalized within 4 hours.
- Severity 3 (Minor Security Vulnerability / Policy Anomaly): Remediation within 14 calendar days.
